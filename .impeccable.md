## Design Context

### Users
Clinicians and authorized care-team members use this responsive, offline-capable wound-progress record to review longitudinal recovery, capture date-specific updates, and manage notes at the correct scope. Demo evaluators also use it to inspect the workflow and data integrity.

### Brand Personality
Calm, trustworthy, and professional. The interface should support focused clinical recordkeeping without implying diagnosis, emergency support, or a certified EHR.

### Aesthetic Direction
An understated light clinical workspace: ink-toned typography, soft blue-green surfaces, restrained borders, clear data hierarchy, and compact controls that hold up on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Avoid decorative density and visual ambiguity.

### Design Principles
- Make record state explicit in text and structure, never color alone.
- Favor calm, compact clarity over attention-seeking decoration.
- Preserve consistent geometry for repeated controls, especially timeline dates.
- Keep hierarchy familiar to care-team workflows: patient, library, date, wound update.
- Maintain accessible labels, contrast, keyboard focus, and responsive layouts.
